analytics Factor Name

The official name or identifier of the emission factor. This describes the specific activity, process, or product that generates emissions.

functions Emission Value

The quantified amount of emissions (typically in CO₂ equivalent). This is the key metric that shows how much greenhouse gas is released for a given unit of activity.

straighten Unit of Measurement

The measurement unit for the emission factor (e.g., kg CO₂/kWh, g CO₂/km). It defines what activity quantity the emission value refers to (per kilowatt-hour, per kilometer, per ton, etc.).

cloud Target Gas

The specific greenhouse gas being measured. Common gases include CO₂ (carbon dioxide), CH₄ (methane), N₂O (nitrous oxide), or CO₂e (CO₂ equivalent, accounting for all GHGs).

location_on Country / Region

The geographical location where this emission factor applies. Emission factors can vary significantly by country due to differences in energy sources, regulations, and industrial practices.

calendar_today Reference Year

The year for which this data is valid or when it was last updated. Emission factors change over time as energy grids become cleaner or production methods improve.

category Emission Scope

Classification of emissions into categories: Scope 1 (direct emissions from owned sources), Scope 2 (indirect emissions from energy consumption), or Scope 3 (other indirect emissions in the value chain).

business Data Publisher

The organization or institution that published or maintains this emission factor data. This helps you evaluate the credibility and sourcing of the information.

description Source Document

The original research paper, report, or official document from which this emission factor was derived. You can review the source to understand the methodology and assumptions used.

link Unique Identifier (IRI)

A unique web identifier (Internationalized Resource Identifier) in RDF format that uniquely identifies this emission factor in the CFKG knowledge graph. It enables semantic linking and data integration.

label Classification Tags

Semantic labels that categorize and classify the emission factor for easier discovery and organization. Tags help group related emission factors by activity type, industry, or methodology.

timeline Historical Evolution

A time-series chart and table showing how the emission factor has changed over multiple years. This reveals trends and helps identify when significant methodology or technology changes occurred.
